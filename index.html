About个人简介

01

I completed my PhD at the University of Nottingham in June 2026, specialising in dialogic teaching practices across cultural contexts. I hold a bachelor’s degree in English (linguistics and phonetics) from China and a master’s degree in TESOL from Canada, and my research is grounded in classroom experience: I taught in a Chinese international school before starting the PhD. 我于 2026 年 6 月在英国诺丁汉大学获得博士学位，研究方向为跨文化情境下的对话教学实践。本科毕业于中国（英语专业，语言学与语音方向），硕士毕业于加拿大（TESOL 第二外语教学）。我的研究扎根于真实课堂经验——攻读博士前，我曾在中国国际学校任教。

I have worked across several research teams on projects that examine educational dynamics comparatively, published in peer-reviewed venues, and present regularly at the American Educational Research Association (AERA), the British Educational Research Association (BERA), the Comparative and International Education Society (CIES) and the World Council of Comparative Education Societies (WCCES). My interests run from teacher education and pedagogy to comparative studies, case study methodology and the sociology of education. 我先后参与多个研究团队，围绕教育互动与比较教育开展研究，成果发表于同行评审期刊与国际出版社，并定期在美国教育研究协会（AERA）、英国教育研究协会（BERA）、比较与国际教育学会（CIES）、世界比较教育学会联合会（WCCES）等国际学术会议上宣读论文。研究兴趣涵盖教师教育、教学法研究、比较教育、个案研究方法与教育社会学。

I believe educational research earns its keep when it changes what happens between a teacher and a class. My fellowship at the Centre for Childhood & Youth Studies at Salem State University extends this into children’s rights — work I intend to keep building on internationally. 我相信，教育研究的价值最终体现在它能否改变教师与学生之间真实发生的事。在美国塞勒姆州立大学儿童与青年研究中心的访问学者研究，将这一关切延伸至儿童权利领域，也是我希望长期在国际范围内持续深耕的方向。
